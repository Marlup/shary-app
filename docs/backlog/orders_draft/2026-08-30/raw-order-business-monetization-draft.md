# Raw Order Backup — Business & Monetization Draft

Date: 2026-08-30

Direct backup of the instruction to produce and persist a complete business
draft for Shary.

## Verbatim scope of the order

1. Scan/read the project briefly to understand its intention, then propose an
   income strategy (subscription, pay-per-usage, others) grounded in popular and
   official theoretical documentation for this kind of app.
2. Produce a resource-usage-limit investigation showing how much free vs paying
   users can consume under pessimistic / conservative / optimistic profiles.
   Context: cloud-based sharing of Fields and data; users do not need to
   register online to use the app as a vault on their phone.
3. Save the result.
4. Extend with:
   - Pros / cons of the monetization approaches.
   - Legal matters in Spain/Madrid about data, user types, and type of
     company / business.
   - Minimal and advanced aspects to tie and cover to make this businessable.
   - Hard frozen limits and usage of data on user types.
   - Costs per one user, 100, 1k, 10k, 100k and 1M users.
   - Critical items to maintain the cloud infrastructure to ensure the service
     (do not count the Google Cloud Services SLA itself; only mention what to
     cover).
   - Other aspects that may be missing.
5. Requirements for the draft: maximum coverage, sufficient components,
   non-overkill, safe and with safeguards at issues.
6. Provide 3-5 protocols / procedures for business interruption, business
   closure, and users whose data could become unavailable or compromised
   (business bankruptcy or disruption).
7. Base answers on official documentation where possible, not on assumptions;
   take time to find reasonable answers and to criticise.
